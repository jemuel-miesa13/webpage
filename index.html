Pokémon Crystal Neptune

What actually happened in Pokémon Crystal Neptune?

Pokémon Crystal Neptune has been on hold for roughly two months. The last public update was on August 23, when I released a demo. When the project was first introduced in June, it was called Pokémon Crystal Aurora. As development continued, I made several major refinements, reworked parts of the music, and redesigned the title screen.

Throughout that period, I became exhausted from my schoolwork and found it increasingly difficult to make time for the project, even on Saturdays and Sundays. I started losing hope and overthinking what the people waiting for the game might think. I had promised that I would release Pokémon Crystal Neptune, but so far, I had only managed to publish the demo. At times, I even worried that the overworld I had worked on might somehow hate me for not fulfilling that promise.

Next week, I have five free days—Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. I plan to use that time to make meaningful progress throughout the project. At the same time, I cannot forget about school. I need to keep up with my responsibilities and make time for my education so that I can graduate. This project matters to me, but my education matters too.

I am hoping to shape Pokémon Crystal Neptune into a Christmas special game. That goal gives me something to work toward, while also reminding me to balance development with school and everything else I need to accomplish.

?Q&A About Crystal Neptune

Why am I stuck on a white screen?

This may happen when the emulator is using the wrong save or ROM type. Change the ROM Type / Save Type setting to Flash 128K, then try launching the patched game again.

Is this another Johto remake game?

Yes, but it takes a different approach and has its own style. Some familiar places have been reworked after many years. Team Aqua and Team Magma have moved to Johto and are stirring up a new mess. Team Rocket, meanwhile, seems to have taken a different path: selling various kinds of Poké Balls and enjoying a quieter life, almost as though they were never villains.

How far does the game go? Is it completed?

Unfortunately, the August 23 build is not complete. It still needs additional scripts and further development. Ecruteak City is the final stop in the current story progression. Other locations, including Olivine City, Routes 38 and 39, Route 42, and Mahogany Town, may be accessible, but they do not yet have their planned person events or additional storyline content.

Pokémon Crystal Neptune is a fan-made ROM-hacking project. You must provide your own original Pokémon Emerald base ROM; this website distributes only the patch file.

About D-Player

D-Player was a project associated with Spaceillusion Inc. that was discontinued following the shutdown in 2012. Although the project is no longer active, some of its related files and programs have been preserved.

▣

Project Background

D-Player is part of a legacy digital project connected with Spaceillusion Inc. Its services or development came to an end in 2012.

⌘

Preserved Materials

Some files and programs are still in the possession of Jemuel Miesa, Richmond Francisco, and another user known as f8rinoa, whose identity is unknown.

✧Keeping a digital archive

The remaining materials offer a way to revisit and study parts of the project's history. This page serves as an informational overview; it does not imply that D-Player is currently maintained or officially operating.
